# Neat Edges

Stop soil and water washing a ragged fringe across your floors.

RimWorld fades every terrain one tile into whatever it touches, and the
receiving tile gets no say in it — which is why a clean stone terrace meeting
open ground always looks like it is dissolving at the rim. Bridges are the one
thing in the game that escape it, and only because they are bridges.

Neat Edges gives you that clean line anywhere.

## What you get

Two things in **Architect → Floors**:

- **Hard edge** — hardens one edge of a tile. Rotate to pick which. Stack
  several on one tile for any combination of edges.
- **Hard edge (all sides)** — one placement, the whole tile.

Nothing is drawn on the map. The floor you already laid simply meets the
boundary cleanly instead of being washed over.

**Corners close themselves.** Where two hardened edges meet, the corner between
them is sealed too — and a run terminates crisply rather than the fringe curling
around its end.

**Free of materials.** It changes how an edge draws and nothing else, so it
costs nothing to place. It still takes a moment of work, so a mis-dragged run
can be cancelled like any other order.

**An overlay toggle** on the bottom-right row shows every hard edge on the map.
An invisible thing you cannot find is an invisible thing you cannot remove.

## Compatibility

Works with any floor, from any mod, and alongside vanilla bridges. Requires
[Harmony](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2009463077).

Safe to add or remove at any time. Nothing is stored in your save beyond the
markers themselves; removing the mod removes them and their effect, and leaves
your floors and terrain untouched.

If you also run **Fine Establishments**, its floor border strips harden the edge
they hug automatically while this is installed.
