v1.1.1 update:

Tiles that NANAME Floors or Smooth Terrain split diagonally now draw a crisp diagonal under a hard edge or border, instead of a soft blend.

A hard edge or border that stops partway no longer hardens the open edge past its end.

NANAME Floors and Smooth Terrain are now on the recommended list.

Hard edges and borders you have already built load as they are and harden the same edges.
